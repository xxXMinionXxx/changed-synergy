# Changed: Synergy 1.1.5-Alpha.2

### Interface

- Highlighted wheel options move slightly outward, enlarge and fade between brightness states. The current ability's activation highlight also fades in and out.
- Redesigned the relationship and right-click social panels with the supplied artwork and title icons. Latex panels follow the displayed creature's theme; human and organic panels use vanilla inventory colors. Text adapts to light and dark backgrounds, and faction labels retain their faction colors.
- Updated the outpost panel with vanilla inventory borders and an inset member list. Creature inventories use vanilla slots and a continuous Work Cargo frame, with a more compact cargo row and a centered label beside it.

### Structures

- Added naturally generated ocean laboratories, white laboratory towers, red laboratory towers and green laboratory towers, **built by Crossader**. Green laboratories generate in jungle, sparse jungle and bamboo jungle. Red towers and green laboratories require Addon Plus. The buildings retain their books, passwords, traps and residents, with loot in previously empty chests and barrels.

### Gameplay

- Added the female Latex Muntjac and synthesizer gender selection for Muntjac transfur items.
- Swordfish Rush now ignores half the target's armor while retaining armor toughness and other protection.
- Non-bonded takeover offers **Give up completely**, with reactions and farewells from the carrier. Fatal consciousness loss gradually covers the screen in the carrier's theme color before death; latex-totem protection still applies.
- Leaving initial native-origin selection with Esc defaults to a human instead of disconnecting. Cancelling an optional reselection keeps the existing identity.
- When monster combat interrupts a chase game, the creature gives a warning instead of a generic end-of-game line.
