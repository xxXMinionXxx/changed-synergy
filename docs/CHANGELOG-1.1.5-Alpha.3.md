# Changed: Synergy 1.1.5-Alpha.3

### Interface

- Updated the mod icon to the supplied Silk Moth artwork.

### Structures

- Applied Crossader's latest laboratory revisions, including updated vaults, loot and lore. Corrected tiered loot-table paths and added the Swordfish Research disc to the aquatic facility's upper research-room loot while preserving its water and layout.
- Revised laboratory books, notes and discs, expanded Muntjac and Swordfish research, and filled four blank records. White-latex research describes retrieving transformed staff and growing eyes outside their territory. Updated the green laboratory's crystal display to a large Dark Latex Crystal on a Dark Latex Block. Existing signatures and puzzle clues remain.
- Adjusted note and clipboard text for their original screens, retaining fuller CRISSCROSS and temple-specimen logs.

### Gameplay

- Added **Friendly hug** mode to the existing player grab ability, toggled by right-clicking its wheel option. Humans, humanoid monsters, latex creatures and transformed players can be held without damage, transfur, absorption or wrapping. Manual release, player escape and existing hostility are preserved, with separate settings for friendly hugs and player targets. Forms without grabbing do not gain the ability.
- Added two pup carry styles: a face-up cradle and an upright carry with tucked forepaws and a hanging tail. Added tailored poses for small feral forms, Feral Sharks, Milk Puddings and Headless Knights. Swordfish and both Muntjac forms smoothly raise and lower their arms when grabbing; the friendly-hug heart badge is fully visible.
- Fixed missing arm-grab escape QTEs, including friendly holds, without requiring a rejoin. Fixed released targets briefly snapping back into a friendly hug, stale held poses and unintended pup poses.
- Friendly hugs now end the current held-key activation when released and apply a two-second recapture cooldown, including NPC targets and manual releases. Holding the release key no longer starts another hug.
- Fixed human players repeatedly triggering creature dialogue while holding the pat key, including Addon Plus's pat key. Continuous pats keep their animation without repeating social reactions when the cursor briefly slips off the target.
- Aquatic creatures can play chase games with players and one another underwater, using swimming navigation at different depths. Existing roles, countdowns, right-click catches and the three-crouch exit remain available; leaving the water or needing air ends the game safely.
- NPC Swordfish can repeat their underwater rush on a four-second cooldown, including at close range.
- Added a dedicated Swordfish Rush death message using the attacker's and victim's names: "Latex Swordfish impaled Oroshiagito by accident."
- Fixed the armor-calculation conflict with Apothic Attributes, preserving its damage formula and penetration attributes alongside Swordfish Rush.
- Giving up during takeover now requires a separate confirmation that can be cancelled. Fatal consciousness loss takes seven seconds to cover the screen, followed by three seconds at full color, allowing farewell dialogue to finish.
- Added advancements for completed land and aquatic chase games, companion boarding, Silk Moth silk production, Swordfish Rush hits, laboratory exploration, voluntary transfur and latex-totem takeover reversal.
